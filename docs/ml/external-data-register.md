# Official external data register

Research date: 2026-09-10. This register records candidates, limitations, and adoption decisions for the first segment travel-time model. It is not evidence that a source has already been ingested. URLs and metadata must be revalidated and snapshotted in a dataset manifest when a source is used.

City of Edmonton portal datasets use the [Open Government Licence — Edmonton, version 1.0](https://data.edmonton.ca/stories/s/City-of-Edmonton-Open-Data-Terms-of-Use/msh8-if28/) unless the individual dataset says otherwise. Socrata datasets are accessible as bulk exports and through `https://data.edmonton.ca/resource/<dataset-id>.json`; production backfills must store query parameters, retrieval timestamp, response checksum, and licence/source attribution.

## Adopt for the first model

| Source | Coverage and cadence | Spatial detail | Access / licence | Modeling value and decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Environment and Climate Change Canada historical climate data](https://climate.weather.gc.ca/) | Search/download of past Canadian hourly, daily, and monthly observations; station availability varies through time. Current official observations identify Edmonton Blatchford as the Edmonton station, but the station ID and hourly continuity must be confirmed before backfill. | Weather-station point observations; city-level context rather than road-segment conditions. | Official search/bulk download; subject to the [ECCC data licence](https://climate.weather.gc.ca/prods_servs/attachment1_e.html). | Adopt hourly temperature, precipitation, visibility, wind, and severe-weather indicators when they can be joined strictly at or before prediction time. Persist station and quality flags. Weather is credible context, not proof of causality. |
| [Alberta general holidays](https://www.alberta.ca/alberta-general-holidays) | Rule-based annual calendar; the official page currently lists dates for 2025–2027. Update/verify annually. | Province-wide. | Official Alberta page; cache a small versioned calendar and retrieval date. | Adopt a holiday/service-day indicator because schedules and traffic differ on holidays. GTFS service calendars remain authoritative for which transit service actually runs. |
| ETS GTFS static and GTFS-Realtime feeds | Static feed in the TransitPulse database covers the schedule/feed validity period; realtime is polled at about 30 seconds and has no official historic backfill identified. | Route/trip/shape/stop topology plus current vehicle/trip/alert state. | Existing official Edmonton endpoints configured in `transitpulse_api.config`; static/realtime snapshots are tied to feed IDs. | Core source. Vehicle observations can label operational movement and reliability, but not boardings, passenger OD, latent demand, walking preference, or mode choice. Keep recording; never fabricate the missing 30-day history. |

## Useful later or as event/context flags

| Source | Coverage and cadence | Spatial detail | Access / licence | Modeling value and decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Traffic Disruptions](https://data.edmonton.ca/widgets/k4tx-5k8p) and [On-street Construction and Maintenance](https://data.edmonton.ca/w/7wiq-4rgy/depj-dfck) | Current/planned disruption records with start/finish fields; historical retention and update cadence were not established from published metadata and must be tested before use. | Mapped road/closure locations or project extents where geometry is present. | City Socrata portal, dataset IDs `k4tx-5k8p` and `7wiq-4rgy`; Edmonton licence. | Potentially useful exogenous flags after spatial intersection with transit shapes and time-valid joins. Do not backfill from today's view and pretend it describes earlier conditions. Adopt only after confirming version/history semantics. |
| [ETS service disruption alerts](https://data.edmonton.ca/w/5yvt-mcye/depj-dfck) and recorded GTFS-RT alerts | Portal view exposes alert identifiers, deletion state, and start timestamps; retained historical completeness/cadence needs validation. TransitPulse already stores latest official GTFS-RT alerts, not necessarily a complete immutable alert history. | Affected routes/stops when supplied. | City portal dataset `5yvt-mcye` or existing GTFS-RT endpoint; Edmonton licence/feed terms. | High-value incident/context flag if a complete as-of history can be established. Never use an alert first published after the prediction timestamp. |
| [ETS route report cards and reports](https://www.edmonton.ca/ets/transit-ridership-reports) | Route report cards are published after service adjustments, which the City says occur five times per year; reports are available for periods from 2021 onward and include boarding information, on-time performance, and frequency. | Route-level aggregates, not public stop-level APC records or passenger OD. | Official City web/PDF reports; usage terms must be checked per document before extraction. | Useful for plausibility checks, route-level demand priors, and later constrained planning. Too coarse and temporally lagged to label individual segment travel time or actual passenger wait. Must not be presented as stop-level demand. |
| [Transit Ridership metric](https://data.edmonton.ca/w/wj6v-epas/depj-dfck) | Official performance metric linked by ETS, but the current visualization is broken/private from the public landing page and its accessible data schema/history could not be verified. | Likely system-level aggregate; no public stop/trip granularity established. | City portal view `wj6v-epas`; Edmonton licence if accessible. | Track as a system trend only. It cannot support OD, transfer, per-stop demand, or candidate passenger-optimal claims. Revalidate endpoint before any use. |
| [Statistics Canada 2021 Edmonton CMA Census Profile](https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/details/page.cfm?DGUIDlist=2021S0503835&GENDERlist=1&HEADERlist=0&Lang=E&STATISTIClist=1&SearchText=edmonton) | 2021 Census, 25% sample commuting variables; multi-year snapshot, not an operational time series. | CMA/profile geography; finer Census geographies may be downloadable but are not stop-level trips. | Official Statistics Canada profile/download under Statistics Canada terms. | Later broad demographic/accessibility context. It reports commuting mode/duration but is not a current passenger OD matrix and must not be used to optimize individual transit trips as if it were one. |

## Investigated and rejected for the first model

| Source | Why rejected now |
|---|---|
| [Average Annual Weekday Traffic Volumes 2011–2022](https://data.edmonton.ca/Transportation/Average-Annual-Weekday-Traffic-Volumes-2011-2022-/b58q-nxjr) | Historical annual average volumes at count locations, based partly on short seasonal counts/estimation. It has no contemporaneous 2026 speed/incident signal and would add a stale road-class proxy rather than the desired traffic dynamics. It may later support static corridor characterization after a leakage-safe spatial join. |
| [Speed Survey Data — 2018](https://data.edmonton.ca/Vehicle-Speed/Speed-Survey-Data-2018/r2jj-3sq9) | Historical-only 2018 survey. The City explicitly warns it is not traffic-counter data; its aggregation/range bins and age make it unsuitable as 2026 segment-speed context. |
| [Annual Collision Report: Collision Severity](https://data.edmonton.ca/w/77sf-j5rj/depj-dfck) | Annual safety aggregate, not a timely incident stream. It cannot explain a particular bus traversal and risks a misleading causal feature. A separate timestamped incident feed would need to be found and validated. |
| Media releases / general event calendars | Incomplete, prose-heavy, and publication time does not reliably equal event start/end or geographic impact. Do not scrape as a training feature. A future official structured major-venue/event source may be registered if it provides time, attendance/capacity, location, history, and stable access. |

## Material gaps

- No official public historical road-segment speed feed was found in this audit. TransitPulse's buses can reveal congestion experienced by buses, but this is not general traffic speed and must not be labeled as such.
- No public stop-level APC/boarding/alighting history, passenger load by trip, fare-card OD, or full travel-demand matrix was found. ETS route report cards are the best official public demand evidence identified, but remain aggregate.
- No reliable official historical collision/incident stream with occurrence timestamp, clearance timestamp, and geometry was established.
- Because those gaps are material, the first target remains operational segment travel time. Initial optimization, once justified, is limited to reliability, regularity, schedule robustness, headway/fleet/recovery decisions, and explicitly labeled wait proxies.
